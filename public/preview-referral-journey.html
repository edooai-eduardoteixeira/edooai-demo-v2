Strategy details
Reward structure
$75 per side ($150 total per successful conversion). Paid only when referee completes first transaction.
Reward types
Account Credit vs. Cashback — A/B tested per user
Customer targeting
Customers ranked by expected ROI: (propensity to refer × expected referee conversion × LTV) minus reward cost. Top-ranked activated first.
Personalization variables
  • Channel: per user's most responsive channel (email, push, SMS, WhatsApp)
  • Timing: peak engagement time per user (inferred from historical activity)
  • Message: 2 variants A/B tested, winner scaled
  • Reward type: Account Credit vs. Cashback, personalized per user preference signals
Offer window
Referee has 14 days from invite to complete first transaction. Expired offers free budget back to the pool.
